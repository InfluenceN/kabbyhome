# Vanity assortment and shopping structure
Researched October 21, 2026.

## Implemented
Three style families, six color variants, 24 existing sample SKUs. Color groups keep matching finishes together; filters cover color, width, finish appearance, sink layout and sample stock. Product rows distinguish appearance from unconfirmed cabinet core and door materials. Existing saved plans and SKU IDs are preserved.

## Popular styles
[2026 U.S. Houzz Bathroom Trends Study](https://st.hzcdn.com/static/econ/2026U.S.HouzzBathroomTrendsStudy.pdf), page 54: Shaker 46%, flat-panel 30%, raised-panel 15%, reeded/fluted 4%. Survey of 1,619 U.S. homeowners on Houzz, fielded August 2026, covering recent/current/planned renovations. These are survey choices, not continent-wide sales shares. Recommendation: retain Shaker, evaluate flat-panel next, test reeded as a smaller accent range.

[NKBA’s 2026 North American professional forecast](https://nkba.org/press/nkba-kbis-releases-annual-2026-bath-trends-report/) identifies transitional/timeless design as popular over the next three years (70% of respondents). Forecasts and completed purchasing behavior are different measures.

## Materials other than solid wood
- Foil or laminate over engineered board: a practical candidate for contemporary flat fronts. [IKEA’s US ÄNGSJÖN vanity](https://www.ikea.com/us/en/p/aengsjoen-backsjoen-bathroom-vanity-with-sink-faucet-high-gloss-white-brown-walnut-effect-s99536366/) specifies particleboard and plastic foil. MDF and particleboard remain wood-derived; this is not a wood-free option.
- PVC-board body with glass fronts: [Eviva manufacturer specification hosted by Home Depot](https://images.thdstatic.com/catalog/pdfImages/ae/ae46638f-5fdf-4d81-b0c4-802d507055b5.pdf) documents a real example. Evaluate board composition, surface, mounting and warranty per model.
- Stainless-steel console: [Signature Hardware Halyard](https://www.signaturehardware.com/24-in-halyard-console-sink-with-modern-top/953616.html) uses a stainless-steel stand and porcelain sink. It offers an open visual profile but less concealed storage.
No reliable North America-wide share ranking for PVC versus metal was found. Availability does not establish popularity; none of these examples establishes Kabby stock.

## Countertops are a separate material decision
[Houzz’s 2026 findings](https://www.houzz.com/magazine/the-bathroom-features-homeowners-want-most-in-2026-stsetivw-vs~185811167): engineered quartz 46%, quartzite 23%, granite and marble 11% each among upgraded vanity countertop choices. Porcelain, ceramic and acrylic are each 2%. Prioritize quartz top compatibility and clearly itemize cabinet-only versus complete-set pricing.

## Assortment recommendation
Keep current six colors under their framed style families. Source a flat-panel range first, comparing a specified engineered-board construction with a true non-wood PVC option. Consider a metal console as a smaller powder-room range. Require supplier construction and finish data before publishing selectable material inventory or waterproof claims. Current stock, prices and geometry remain planning samples.
